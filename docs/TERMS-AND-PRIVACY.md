# MetaSync — Terms & Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-08-11 · Version: v1_

MetaSync is a companion app for the Steam release of MARVEL vs CAPCOM Fighting Collection. It reads game
memory locally to show live match state and applies cosmetic character skins, and — when **Live Sync** is
enabled — connects you to the MetaSync network (leaderboards, ranked stats, and matchup features).

## Using MetaSync offline

You can use MetaSync's local features (skins, live overlay) **without** Live Sync. In offline mode, no data
leaves your machine — but leaderboards, player sync, and stats/metrics are unavailable, since those require
the network.

## What Live Sync collects

When you enable Live Sync, MetaSync collects **match data** and associates it with your **Steam ID**:

- Match results, teams, and per-frame in-match state (positions, health, inputs, and similar gameplay values).
- Your Steam ID and Steam persona name, used to identify your records on the leaderboards.

MetaSync does **not** collect your Steam password, payment information, private messages, files, or any data
outside the game.

## How it's used

- **To operate the service** — power the leaderboards, ranked stats, matchup intel, and player sync.
- **To operate and improve MetaSync** — analyze match data in aggregate to build and refine features.

Because match data is tied to your Steam ID, it is treated as personal data. Your consent to Live Sync is
your permission for the uses above. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning Live Sync off, which
stops all further syncing.

## Sharing

Match data may be visible to other players as part of the service (e.g. leaderboard standings, head-to-head
records). MetaSync does not sell your data.

## Your choices

- **Turn Live Sync off** at any time to stop syncing.
- **Request deletion** of your stored match data by contacting us with your Steam ID.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: **support@nobd.net**.

_This is a beta service; these terms may be updated, and continued use of Live Sync after an update means you
accept the current version._
