Go Live with MetaSync

Live Sync connects you to the MetaSync network — leaderboards, ranked stats, matchup intel, and new features built on real match data.

To power these, MetaSync syncs your match data (associated with your Steam ID) to our servers. You can use MetaSync offline, but without Live Sync there are no leaderboards, player sync, or stats.
