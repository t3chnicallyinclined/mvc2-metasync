⚠ ONLY THE FULL RENDER PRODUCES A VALID NUMBER. Alpha blending is not decomposable, so a subset
  replayed over a zero clear can never equal the composite — under any mask. The subset buttons are
  for LOOKING at a class of draws, not for judging one.

ELIMINATED BY MEASUREMENT (do not re-litigate):
  culling / frontFace   cullNone on vs off differs by 10 px in 696,360. 566 draws take cullMode
                        'front' and survive, so they are back-facing and the mapping is correct.
  depth occlusion       characters sit at NDC z 0.98118, the stage at 0.9992; under LESS_EQUAL the
                        characters always pass. Hand-executing a character quad matches truth 20/20.
  fog                   fFogDensity = 0, fFogColor = (0,0,0), from the properly shadowed buffers.
  stencil               ffunc = ALWAYS with all ops KEEP. Measured, not inferred.

READ COVERAGE FIRST. If pixels are MISSING, that is geometry and no amount of shading work will fix
it. Only once coverage matches does the colour number mean anything.
